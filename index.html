MAT226C · UC Davis

Adaptive Stokesian Dynamics project

Problem: Fixed timesteps caused overlaps/instability in flexible aggregate simulations.
Method: Implemented adaptive RK/Bogacki-Shampine and RKF-style timestepping with error control.
Outcome: Stabilized interparticle dynamics and reduced overlap artifacts in test systems.
